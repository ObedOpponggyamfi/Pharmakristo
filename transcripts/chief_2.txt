Interviewer: What are the major concerns raised by residents in this area?

Elder:
The issue most residents complain about is water shortage. During the dry season, some people walk
long distances to fetch water from streams and boreholes. This affects productivity and health.
Another issue is poverty and unemployment among the youth. Many families depend on farming, but
low crop prices and poor transportation reduce their income significantly.

Interviewer: How does this affect the younger generation?

Elder:
Many young people feel discouraged because they see limited opportunities in the community.
Some migrate to urban areas while others engage in risky activities such as illegal mining.
Parents are worried because unemployment is increasing social problems and reducing school attendance.

Interviewer: What role do chiefs and elders play?

Elder:
Traditional authorities mediate disputes, mobilize residents for development activities, and
maintain social order. We also engage government institutions and NGOs to advocate for schools,
roads, health centers, and youth support programs.
