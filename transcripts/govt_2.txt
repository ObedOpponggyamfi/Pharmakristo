Interviewer: What development concerns are most frequently raised by residents?

District Official:
Residents frequently complain about poor roads, limited healthcare services, and unemployment.
Communities also request more schools, water systems, and agricultural support programs.
The district is trying to address these concerns despite financial limitations.

Interviewer: How serious is youth unemployment?

District Official:
Youth unemployment is one of the biggest social and economic challenges. Some young people
lack employable skills, and there are limited industries within the district. We are currently
exploring vocational training initiatives and partnerships with private organizations.

Interviewer: How would you describe collaboration with traditional authorities?

District Official:
The collaboration is generally good. Chiefs help communicate government policies,
encourage peace, and support community mobilization during development projects.
