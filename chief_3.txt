Interviewer: How would you assess development progress in the community?

Chief:
Development has been slow over the years. Roads are poor, electricity supply is unstable,
and some schools lack adequate learning materials. Farmers continue to face transportation
difficulties because trucks cannot easily access farms during certain seasons.

Interviewer: What are the key priorities for improvement?

Chief:
Education, healthcare, and employment opportunities should be prioritized. We need more investment
in vocational training, agriculture, and youth empowerment programs. Healthcare facilities should
also be expanded because the current clinic lacks equipment and staff.

Interviewer: How is the relationship between traditional leaders and political authorities?

Chief:
Generally, the relationship is positive. Chiefs are willing to cooperate with assembly members,
district officials, and NGOs to support development initiatives. However, communities sometimes
feel disappointed when promises are not fulfilled on time.
