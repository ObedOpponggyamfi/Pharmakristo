Interviewer: What are the current priorities of the district assembly?

Assembly Member:
The district assembly is focusing on road construction, sanitation, education, and water projects.
Several communities have requested improvements in healthcare and youth employment opportunities.
However, budget constraints continue to delay some projects.

Interviewer: How do you engage traditional leaders in development planning?

Assembly Member:
Traditional leaders are consulted during planning meetings because they understand community needs.
Chiefs help mobilize residents for communal labor and assist in resolving local disputes.
Their involvement improves communication between the assembly and the communities.

Interviewer: What are the major governance challenges?

Assembly Member:
Funding limitations are a major challenge. In addition, expectations from residents are increasing,
especially regarding roads, schools, hospitals, and employment opportunities.
